# Data license — toponym dataset

`recheck.json`, `gcps.json`, `curation.json`, `quality_flags.json` and the files
derived from them, including `web/src/data/toponims.json`) — the readings,
modern-name identifications, types, confidence, NGIB matches and georeferenced

## Scope

- This license covers the **data** only.
- The underlying map image (*Insula Maioricae*) is a historical work; the master
  used here is a 1946 reprint (see `docs/vicenc-mut-1683-map.md`).
- Canonical toponym forms come from the **NGIB** (Nomenclàtor Geogràfic de les
  Illes Balears) and remain subject to their own terms.
- The pipeline **code** in this repository is not covered by this data license.
